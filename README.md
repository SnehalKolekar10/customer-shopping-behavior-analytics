# Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics
project focused on understanding consumer purchasing patterns and
identifying insights that can help a retail company improve sales,
customer engagement, satisfaction, and long-term customer loyalty.

The project analyzes customer demographics, product categories,
purchasing behavior, discounts, reviews, seasons, and payment preferences
using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers'
shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction,
and long-term loyalty.

The management team wants to identify purchasing trends across
different demographics, product categories, and customer preferences.

The main business question addressed in this project is:

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify
> trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and
> product strategies?**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze customer shopping behavior
- Understand purchasing patterns across demographics
- Identify popular products and product categories
- Analyze customer preferences
- Study the relationship between discounts and purchases
- Analyze customer review ratings
- Identify preferred payment methods
- Analyze seasonal purchasing patterns
- Identify important customer and product trends
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate actionable business insights
